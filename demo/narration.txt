Prediction markets can contain useful information, but a long catalog still leaves researchers, traders, and newsrooms asking where to look first.

Panta Signal Lens turns the Panta market catalog into an explainable intelligence feed. It ranks every market with a transparent score built from conviction, liquidity, and market phase, then surfaces the strongest signal, the most liquid market, the closest call, and total tracked volume.

The public judge demo is available with clearly labeled sample data. Search, category, and phase filters recalculate the entire view instantly, so a user can narrow attention without losing context.

Under the hood, the same interface connects to the live Panta API through a server-side key. This is a genuine live response from Panta's test environment, including its current market catalog and category taxonomy.

Opening a market combines the detail and recent-trades endpoints. Signal Lens summarizes current probability, volume, and directional YES versus NO flow while preserving the underlying trade evidence.

The integration uses Panta's markets, categories, market detail, and trades routes. Credentials stay server-side, the source is public, and the product visibly attributes Panta wherever its data appears.

Panta Signal Lens is a working foundation for faster, evidence-based market research, powered by Panta.
